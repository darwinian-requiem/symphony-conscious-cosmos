## When the Cold Breaks (Short Film)
A journey through the cosmic void. Three figures navigate an endless mirrored corridor, suspended in isolation. As the boundaries of space and sound dissolve in a blinding flash, the cold finally breaks, giving way to a profound moment of universal connection. This sequence blends immersive sound design and evocative imagery to capture the essence of a shared cosmic existence.
