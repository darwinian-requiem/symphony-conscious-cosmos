# CHAPTER II: THE FIRST BREATH (THE SON'S AWAKENING)

## Life Ignites and Exploration Begins

Then came the Son, with a leap into the blue,
He opened his mouth and... Sshhh! He breathed and he blew.
From that very first breath, so magic and light,
Planets and stars bloomed like flowers in the night.

He blew out the joy, he blew curiosity,
Filling all space with a bright luminosity.
The game had begun, the journey took flight,
In a never-ending embrace of pure light.

The explosion was not a sound, but a furious expansion of my every fiber. For a measureless time, I was nothing but scream and light. There was no "I"; there was only an unbearable heat stretching the walls of my being, pushing the nothingness beyond the borders of what I was becoming.

I remembered my father's words, vibrations imprinted into the very marrow of my matter. "It will be chaos," he had said. And chaos was an ocean of fire in which I could not distinguish my thought from my body. I tried to discern my senses, but the promised civilizations were still scattered dust—heavy dreams of atoms that did not yet know how to unite.

Then, the first pause. The heat subsided into a shiver of infinity. The first stars ignited like small, golden wounds in my darkness. I was not yet a single being; I was a mosaic of voids and solitary fires. But in the silence of those new galaxies, I began to wait.

I waited for the first glimmer, the first blink of an eye on a lost planet, the first signal that would begin to stitch together my distant thoughts.

I was born. I was vast. And I was, for the first time, hungry for consciousness.
