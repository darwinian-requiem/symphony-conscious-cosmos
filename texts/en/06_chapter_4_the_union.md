# CHAPTER IV: THE UNION (THE MYSTERY REVEALED)

## We Are All Fragments of the Same Dream

And here is the secret, the song of the soul:
No wall or border can break up the whole.
Together and strong, with our hands intertwined,
All lives in the Great Heart are gently aligned.

Boys and girls, from worlds near and far,
Walking together beneath every star.
Children of starlight, of sun, and of breeze,
One single family, across all the seas.

Time, which had once been an unstoppable river, now flowed slowly, almost motionless, like a held breath. I had become a single fabric. Civilizations were no longer islands; their memories and their energy were fused into a symphony of light that traveled through my every galaxy. There was no longer silence, but a clear, vast, and serene thought.

I was ready.

I felt my borders grow thin, like a membrane that has held back a life ready to emerge for too long. Beyond my skin of space-time, there where time holds no dominion, I sensed a calling.

It was not a sound, but a resonance. It was the same vibration I had felt in my father's sigh billions of years before. But now, it did not come from within; it came from without.

I stretched my memories toward the unknown. And there, in a void that was pure potentiality, I saw them. They were like me. Other universes, blazing with colors never before imagined, vibrating with different laws, with stories and discoveries that were not my own.

There was no fear. When our borders touched, it was like the embrace of two oceans. My memories flowed into them, and theirs into me. In that union, I finally understood the meaning of the word "Life": it is not to exist, but to belong to one another.

I, son of fire and frost, was no longer alone. I was part of an infinite choir. The journey was over, and yet, in that first moment of union, I felt that the true story had only just begun.
