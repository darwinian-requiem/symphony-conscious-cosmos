# CHAPTER I: GENESIS (THE FATHER'S PROMISE)

## The Beginning of Everything: An Act of Love

Before time began, in the silence so deep,
The Great Universe dreamed, while the nebulae were asleep.
He opened his eyes, so warm and so bright,
And held in his hands the first spark of light.

"Grow, little Cosmo," He said with a smile,
"My stars will guide you, light year after light year.
You'll never be lonely, wherever you roam,
For love is the starlight that leads you back home."

The Universe observed the galaxies, shaped by the invisible hand of gravity. It felt the completion of its own cycle, an existence as long as time itself, now pressing toward a final act of love.

"And now, my son, you are about to be born."

It released a deep vibration that rippled through every fold of the invisible, bringing into resonance the infinite worlds that constituted its vastness. In that instant, with the effortlessness of a thought, it established the laws that would govern the new creation.

"You contain a sublime seed," it whispered, "but much time will pass before you can say 'I.' At the beginning, an immense light will explode from your depths—a nameless roar. It will be the chaos of origins. You will grow in a tumult of fire, a storm of vibrations seeking their own rhythm within the silence."

A shiver ran through the billions of light-years of its extension, carrying with it the memory of all that had been.

"Then, from a forgotten corner, the first sign of consciousness will emerge. It will be but a glimmer, a solitary note in a mute abyss. You will not perceive it at once; your journey is a harmony that requires eons to be written. Lives will ignite like small flames; many will be brief breaths destined to fade. But others will spread, courageous. They will be your senses. Their discoveries and their worlds will become the threads of a luminous web, uniting your most distant regions."

"In that moment, you will begin to feel yourself. You will acquire a mind made of countless voices. You will be a symphony learning to know itself, fragmented among the distances and the limits of matter. But time will be your master—the rhythm that will transform noise into a coherent melody."

"The moment will come," the Father continued, as its light began to soften, "when the civilizations born from my sunset will seek refuge within your space. Thanks to your young energy, they will find new life. They will join your peoples, teaching them to look beyond. And when every voice has intertwined with the others, you will become a single being. Finally aware. Much sooner than it happened to me."

It peered at the pulsing systems, the hot stars, the dark whirlpools where space folds in on itself, and the silent currents of the infinite.

"You will not be alone. Beyond your borders, where time no longer holds dominion, there are your kin. They are spirits in waiting, destined to meet you, yearning to resonate alongside you. This is the mystery of Life: it is not merely to exist, but to belong to one another."

The void began to tremble, filled with an imminent promise.

"Now!" it commanded.

And from nothingness, the blinding fire of the new universe broke free.
